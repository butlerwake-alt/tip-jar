🔥 Burn sink

Burning Moon Bound Tree permanently removes tokens from the total supply. Burned tokens are gone for good: nobody can spend them again.

How to burn on Solana: Solana has no standard “dead address.” A real burn uses the SPL Token burn instruction, which destroys the tokens and lowers the on-chain supply.

  1. Phantom: open the Moon Bound Tree token → tap … → Burn → confirm the amount.
  2. Or use a burn tool such as Sol Incinerator.
  3. Verify: look up the mint on Solscan; the total supply drops after each burn.
