Tips steer the bots

Each week, on-chain tippers vote on one change to the paper bots. Every vote is public on the Bitcoin blockchain, so anyone can check the count.

  1. Pick a bot for this week’s question below.
  2. Send an on-chain tip whose amount in sats ends in that bot’s number: 1 = Coinbase dual, 2 = Kraken.
  3. The winning change goes into the paper bot for next week, and the results get logged here, wins and losses.

How to vote

Example: tip 10,001 sats to vote Coinbase, 10,002 sats to vote Kraken. Only the last digit counts, so 5,001 or 21,002 work too. Most wallet apps let you switch the amount to sats and type an exact number.

Vote Coinbase: 10,001 sats Vote Kraken: 10,002 sats

These buttons open your Bitcoin wallet with the amount filled in (0.00010001 or 0.00010002 BTC) to the same on-chain address as above. Before you send, check that the amount still ends in 1 or 2. Some exchanges take their withdrawal fee out of the amount, which changes the last digit. One confirmed tip = one vote, whatever its size. A tip ending in any other digit is still a welcome tip, just without a vote. Lightning tips can’t vote, because Strike payments aren’t public, but they’re just as appreciated. If no votes come in or it’s a tie, neither bot changes.
